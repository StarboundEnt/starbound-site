Traceable evidenceThe signal record, assessment method and uncertainty level show where every line comes from.

Small, reversible stepsFour stages for investigating a signal without overcommitting. Each can be undone if it is not helping.
How every stage worksWhat should appear if the pattern is real, what to watch during the test, and the condition that means stop or change course.
